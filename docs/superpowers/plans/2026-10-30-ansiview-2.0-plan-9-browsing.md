**Goal:** Deliver roadmap items 38 to 47 (items 35 to 37 shipped in the 16colo.rs previews plan): search inside art (one file, a folder or pack, the whole library), side by side compare with differing cells highlighted, smooth auto scroll and a minimap for tall art, custom palette files switched live, a library with tags, favourites, star ratings and smart folders, artist and group pages from SAUCE (plus 16colo.rs), a duplicate finder, automatic FILE_ID.DIZ / NFO display when a pack opens, and background indexing and thumbnailing that stays fast on 10,000 files.

## Rulings (with cost if wrong)

1. **Search is a substring match, folded.** Case and diacritics are ignored (`ArtSearch.fold`: lowercased, diacritics removed, CP437 already mapped to Unicode). In one file the query is matched inside each row of the finished art (a match does not span rows), runs of spaces in the query match one or more spaces in the art, so a query typed with one space still finds words the artist spaced out. In a folder, pack or the library a query of several words matches a piece when every word appears in its name, SAUCE title, author, group, comments or art text. Cost if wrong: one function each.
2. **Find works on the finished art.** Find in a document window skips a playback in progress to its end first (the frame on screen during playback is not what the artist finished). Cmd-F shows the bar, Return and Cmd-G go to the next match, Shift-Return and Cmd-Shift-G to the previous, Escape closes. Matches are outlined; the current one is filled and scrolled into view. The Edit menu's text editing group is replaced by a Find submenu (Find..., Find Next, Find Previous) whose commands go to the focused art view, and fall back to the standard text finder for text fields. Cost if wrong: Spelling and Substitutions items are no longer in the Edit menu (no AnsiView window uses them).
3. **Compare compares finished screens cell by cell.** Two cells differ when their character, foreground, background or blink differ, except that blank cells (space, NUL, 255) ignore the foreground they cannot show. A cell present in only one piece (different sizes) differs. The window shows both pieces side by side with differing cells tinted red, scrolls them together, counts differences ("1,204 of 2,000 cells differ"), and steps through differing rows with Cmd-G / Cmd-Shift-G. Entry points: File > Compare With... in a viewer, Compare in a gallery, library or duplicate group with two pieces selected. Cost if wrong: animations compare their last frames only.
4. **Auto scroll** moves the view down smoothly at a speed in lines per second (choices 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48; default 8, remembered in the app's own defaults, not the Quick Look shared suite). View > Auto Scroll (Cmd-Shift-A) starts or stops it; while it runs, + / - (or Up / Down) change speed, Space pauses, Escape or a click stops. It stops at the bottom; starting again at the bottom starts from the top. It is offered only when the art is taller than the window. A small bar at the bottom of the window shows the speed with a (?) hint. Cost if wrong: one settings key.
5. **Minimap** is a 64 point strip at the right edge of a document window showing the whole finished art squeezed to the strip (width and height scaled separately, the way code editors draw them) with a box for the visible part. Click or drag jumps there; it appears when the art is at least twice as tall as the window (View > Show Minimap, on by default, remembered) and hides during the 25 line terminal playback window. Cost if wrong: very tall art looks compressed in the strip (it is a navigation aid, not a preview).
6. **Palettes.** A palette is 16 colours in VGA attribute order. Loaders: JASC-PAL text (`JASC-PAL`, `0100`, count, `r g b` lines), GIMP `.gpl` (`GIMP Palette` header, `Name:` and `Columns:` lines, `#` comments, `r g b name` lines), Adobe `.act` (768 bytes of RGB, optionally followed by a big endian colour count and a transparency index), and the embedded palettes of XBin (flag 0x01, 48 bytes of 6 bit RGB) and ADF (64 colour EGA style table, entries 0,1,2,3,4,5,20,7,56..63). Fewer than 16 colours are padded with VGA, more are cut to 16. Switching remaps every cell whose colour is one of the piece's own 16 colours (its XBin or ADF palette, else VGA) to the same index of the chosen palette; other colours (24 bit, 256 colour cube) stay. The choice applies to the focused window at once (playback continues, every frame is remapped) and becomes the default for new app windows; loaded files are copied into the container (`Application Support/Palettes`). View > Palette lists "Art's Own Colours", the built in "VGA", "CGA (dark yellow)" and "Greyscale", and loaded palettes; Next Palette is Cmd-Option-P. Cost if wrong: a 24 bit cell that happens to equal a VGA colour is remapped too (cells store colours, not indexes).
7. **Library storage.** `Application Support/Library/library.json` (user data: folders with bookmark data, tags, favourites, ratings, smart folders; small, written atomically on change) and `Application Support/Library/index.json` (scan records; rewritten after each scan batch). User data is keyed by the piece's content identity (`ArtFile.identity`), so tags follow a file through renames and moves and a copy carries its tags. Folders are kept as app scoped security bookmarks; a stale bookmark is refreshed when it resolves, a folder that cannot be resolved shows "Folder not found" and its records stay until the user removes it. Cost if wrong: exact copies share their tags and ratings.
8. **Smart folders** are named rule lists, "all" or "any": tag is / tag is not, rating at least, is favourite, text contains, title / author / group contains, format is, year from / to (SAUCE date), folder is. Built in: Favourites, Rated 4 Stars or More, Untagged, Recently Added (30 days). Cost if wrong: rules are data; adding a field is one case.
9. **Keyboard in the library:** Cmd-F search, 0 to 5 set the rating of the selected pieces, F toggles favourite, T edits tags, Return opens, Space previews (opens the slideshow at that piece). Every control has a tooltip; jargon (SAUCE, smart folder, rendered cell hash) has a (?) button that shows a short explanation.
10. **Indexing** runs on a background queue at utility priority, a few files at a time, never on the main thread. A file is read again only when its size or modification date changed (packs: the ZIP's). Each record keeps: path (for a pack entry, the ZIP path plus the entry path), size, modification date, full SHA-256 of the bytes, rendered cell hash, SAUCE title / author / group / date / comments, format, columns, rows, and the art's text (the first 20,000 characters of `ArtText.plainText`). Thumbnails are rendered by the indexer into `Caches/Thumbnails` (keyed by path, size and date; 300 MB cap with the existing `DiskCache` eviction) so galleries and the library show them at once later. ZIP files inside added folders are indexed as packs. Scans run when the library opens, when a folder is added, and on Rescan; there is no file system watching. Cost if wrong: a change made while the library is open shows after Rescan.
11. **Galleries render visible thumbnails first.** The pieces on screen go first, then the rest in order; a disk thumbnail (from the cache) is used without reading the file. Cost if wrong: one ordering function.
12. **Duplicates.** Two kinds of group: "Exact copies" (same SHA-256 of the whole file) and "Same art" (same rendered cell hash, different bytes: SAUCE, line endings or redundant codes differ). The cell hash is SHA-256 over the finished screen's width and cells (character, colours, blink, Unicode scalar), with trailing blank rows dropped, NUL and 255 drawn as space, and a blank cell's invisible foreground ignored. Empty art (no visible cell) never groups. The review window lists groups with every copy's path, size and kind; nothing is ticked at first; "Tick All but One" keeps the first in each group (shortest path, then oldest); Move to Trash asks for confirmation naming the count and uses the Trash (`FileManager.trashItem`), never deletes. Entries inside ZIP packs are listed but cannot be ticked ("inside a pack"). Cost if wrong: one grouping function.
13. **Pack info.** When a gallery opens a ZIP pack or a folder that contains `FILE_ID.DIZ` (any case, any folder depth, the shallowest first) or `.NFO` files, a side pane shows them at once, rendered as art, with a picker when there are several (DIZ first, then NFOs by name). View > Show Pack Info (Cmd-Option-I) hides or shows it; the choice is remembered. In the 16colo.rs browser, selecting a pack previews its FILE_ID file when nothing else is selected (one request through the lane, cancelled by any other selection). Cost if wrong: one setting.
14. **Artist and group pages** come from the library's SAUCE authors and groups (trimmed, case folded; shown in the spelling used most). A page lists the pieces, the years (SAUCE dates), the groups the artist appears with (or the artists of the group). "On 16colo.rs" loads, on request, `/v1/artist/<name>` or `/v1/group/<name>?packs=true` (lowercased name, percent encoded) and lists packs by year; double click opens the pack in the 16colo.rs browser's download path (downloaded and shown in a gallery). An empty answer or HTTP 404 says "16colo.rs has nothing listed under this name". Cost if wrong: names that 16colo.rs spells differently are not found.
