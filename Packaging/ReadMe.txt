AnsiView
========

View classic BBS ANSI art on your Mac, with true VGA colors, block graphics,
animations that play back at modem speed, and blinking text.

INSTALL

1. Drag AnsiView onto the Applications folder.
2. Open AnsiView once from Applications. This turns on the Quick Look preview.
   (You can close the Open dialog that appears.)

USE

- In Finder, select any .ANS, .ICE, .DIZ, .NFO, .ASC, .XB, .IDF, .PCB or .AVT
  file and press the spacebar.
- View > CRT Effect adds scanlines and glow.
- Or double click the file to open it in the AnsiView app.
- Click the art to skip an animation to its end, or click again to replay it.
- In the app, press space to pause, Command ] and Command [ to change speed.
- File > Export Image or Export Animation saves PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or MP4.
- In the app, the Playback menu sets the modem speed (300 baud to Instant).
  The spacebar preview uses the same speed.

UNINSTALL

Drag AnsiView from Applications to the Trash.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
